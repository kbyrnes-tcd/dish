# dish
Dish is a gamified food discovery web app designed to help users try new restaurants in Dublin.

Users select preferences such as cuisine, location, price range, and course type. With one click, the app recommends a specific restaurant and dish to try.

After completing a “Dish”, users can upload a photo, leave a review, and earn experience points. As users level up, they unlock themed avatars and backgrounds tied to different cuisines, encouraging cuisine/city exploration and repeat engagement.

Dish removes decision fatigue from dining by turning food discovery into a game.
